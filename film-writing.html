Manuscript Preview: Chapter I

The rain over the city didn’t clear the air; it only dragged the smoke lower, pinning it against the wet asphalt of the narrow alleyways.

Inside the office, the overhead light hummed a flat, metallic note. I watched the cigarette smoke drift lazily across the desk, moving like a slow fog rolling into a harbor. It was always the small things that stayed in place while everything else tore apart at the seams. The wooden table between us was solid, heavy, marked with deep rings from old cups and older arguments.

She hadn't spoken for three minutes. She didn't look at me; her eyes remained focused entirely on the shattered porcelain piece sitting in the palm of her hand. It was a fragment of a design that didn’t belong to this city, a piece of a history carved out somewhere far away and carried across coastlines in an old leather trunk. "They think it's gone," she said finally, her voice barely rising over the low hum of the street below. "But secrets don't vanish. They just get built over."

I leaned back in the chair, letting the shadow of the window frame cut diagonally across the paperwork. Outside, the neon lights flashed an artificial, hollow blue against the glass...
