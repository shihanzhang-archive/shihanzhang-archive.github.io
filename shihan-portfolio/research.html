Peer-Reviewed Publications

Solar Engineering Thermal Dynamics
Zhang, S. C., & Reynolds, M. T. (2026). "Optimizing Airflow Distribution Profiles Within Specialized Pentagonal Beehive Solar Thermal Matrices." Journal of Renewable Atmospheric Systems, 14(2), 112–128.

Abstract

This inquiry addresses ventilation efficiency limits found inside modular heating grids. By standardizing a highly replicable pentagonal assembly pattern, our physical simulations demonstrated a marked improvement in localized solar collection velocity. The resulting data points to a major structural alternative for balancing winter thermal collection profiles while limiting baseline structural installation overhead.

Hakka Material Culture Domestic Labor Mapping
Zhang, S. C. (2025). "The Geography of Silence: Spatial Layouts and Transgenerational Labor Erasure in Fujian Tulou Compounds." International Review of Historical Sociology, 29(4), 341–359.

Abstract

This study tracks how physical architectural design layouts quietly reinforce social expectations and divide domestic labor across generations inside standard Fujian Hakka circular compounds. Using spatial layout diagrams alongside historical interviews, this paper details the hidden daily operations maintained entirely by female family lines, framing their collective strategies as key actions of agency and community building.

Ecological Modeling Soil Architecture
Zhang, S. C., & Lee, K. Y. (Manuscript in Review). "Tracing Structural Longevity: Organic Material Dependencies and Topographical Soil Diagnostics in Coastal Eco-Systems."

Abstract

This paper investigates the immediate physical feedback loops running between natural vegetation systems and regional architectural preservation settings. Through data gathered in field samples across coastal regions, our analytics track how changing soil chemistry directly impacts foundation stability thresholds over extended periods.
