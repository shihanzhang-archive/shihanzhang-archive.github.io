Concept I // Human

Ngìn · 人

An exploration of female erasure, ancestral memory, and domestic labor within multicultural contexts.

7 Seconds Memory, 2025
Mixed Media Collage on Canvas

This piece uses a stream-of-consciousness approach that feels like a conversation between fragmented memories, showing how migration, memory, and identity can break apart while still remaining deeply connected. A cruise ship representing a new life in Florida collides with a traditional Chinese wooden table.

Can we smoke here?, 2025
Linoleum Cut Print on Mulberry Paper

A crowded household scene where men casually play cards as cigarette smoke drifts from their side of the room into a cramped kitchen packed with women preparing food. The men occupy space freely while the women remain confined to labor.

Hands that Feed, Hands that Burn, 2025
Mixed Media and Woodcut Overlay

A man holds a cigarette whose burning cherry replaces where a woman’s head should be. Her body is gone, leaving only an empty apron behind. Bright colors emphasize how women are often reduced to roles that erase individuality.
